# Study Corner 🖥️

A simple **3D Computer Graphics mini project** made with **C++ and OpenGL**.

### Features

* 3D study room with desk, chair, bookshelf, window, and monitor
* 3D transformations: translation, rotation, and scaling
* Point light and directional light
* Rotating ceiling fan
* Falling rain animation
* Different colors and materials
* Camera movement and rotation

### Controls

* **W / A / S / D** — Move camera
* **Arrow Keys** — Look around
* **1** — Toggle desk lamp
* **2** — Toggle sunlight
* **F** — Start/stop fan
* **ESC** — Exit

### Run

Open `StudyCorner.sln` in **Visual Studio**, select **Debug + x64**, and press **F5**.
